# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Wide Rules harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming for everyone regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus on technical accuracy, reproducible evidence, and respectful peer review.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect project scope, security disclosure timelines, and licensing terms.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information or submitting intentionally malicious rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove unacceptable contributions or restrict participation for violations reported through the repository issue or advisory channels.
